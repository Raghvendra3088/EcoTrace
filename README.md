# EcoTrace — Carbon Footprint Tracker (SDG 13: Climate Action) 🌍 

SDG 13 — Climate Action
Climate change is the defining challenge of our generation. Global average temperatures have risen more
than 1.1°C above pre-industrial levels, causing extreme weather events, melting glaciers, rising sea levels,
and disrupting ecosystems worldwide. The UN SDG 13 calls for urgent and ambitious action to combat
climate change and its impacts by 2030.
LIVE link : https://raghvendra3088.github.io/EcoTrace/

Why This SDG Was Selected -

• Climate change affects every human being — from farmers facing droughts to coastal communities
facing floods.

• Individual carbon emissions account for a significant share of global greenhouse gas output.

• Most people have no quantitative awareness of their personal carbon footprint.

• Awareness and data-driven nudges can measurably change behaviour and reduce emissions.

# Working features in the app:

1. Carbon Calculator — 4 tabs (Transport, Energy, Food, Waste) with real emission factors
2. Live Score Panel — shows total CO₂, breakdown bars, eco rating (A+ to F), and personalized tips
3. Analytics Dashboard — KPI cards, bar trend chart, donut category chart
4. 6_Reduction Tips — with impact estimates per category
5. Responsive design — works on mobile and desktop
